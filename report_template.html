      Limits of this analysis

      • Survivorship. Seven IPOs from the list (Olaplex, AvidXchange, Informatica, Udemy, HashiCorp, Zeekr, OneStream) are missing because the Zeus database only holds stocks that still trade. SK hynix (an already‑public company's US listing) and the Pershing Square USA fund are excluded as not being normal IPOs.
      • Small, clustered sample. 96 stocks at most, and only 76 with a full six months. Many listed in two hot windows (late 2021 and mid‑2025) that both reversed sharply. So these results partly describe those two market cycles, not just IPOs in general.
      • Not risk‑adjusted. "vs SPY" is a simple difference in returns. New IPOs are 5–6× as volatile as SPY, so this is not a beta‑adjusted alpha.
      • Big‑deal bias. The list is limited to IPOs worth about $1B or more. Small‑cap IPOs, biotech IPOs and SPAC mergers can behave very differently.
      • Data fixes. RENT and BIRD prices were corrected for 1‑for‑20 reverse splits. Stub first bars for BTGO and ETOR were dropped. KLAR, IBTA, CRBG and INIO are missing their real first day, so they're left out of the day‑1 and pop statistics.
      • Not a trading signal as is. Shorting new IPOs is often expensive or impossible (high borrow fees, few shares to borrow), and the right tail is brutal: CoreWeave rose about 200% in its first six months.
