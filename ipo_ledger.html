Notes

How to read this

  • Scope. These are operating companies with roughly $1B or more in value at IPO, or about $500M+ raised. The full universe is much larger: 2021 alone had about 1,000 US listings, most of them SPACs. For every name, including micro‑caps and SPACs, see stockanalysis.com.
  • Valuation is the approximate market value at the offer price. Sources differ on whether they count basic or fully diluted shares, so figures can vary by 10–20%. “n/d” means I couldn't confirm a figure.
  • Raised is the base deal size in USD and does not include the greenshoe.
  • Deal type. A carve‑out is a subsidiary floated by its parent. An ADR listing means the company already traded at home (for example SK hynix in Korea). Direct listings raised no new capital.
  • 2025–2026 figures come from Renaissance Capital, IPOScoop, company press releases and news coverage as of Sep 2026. Earlier figures are from widely reported pricing data. Check any number in the S‑1/424B4 before relying on it.
